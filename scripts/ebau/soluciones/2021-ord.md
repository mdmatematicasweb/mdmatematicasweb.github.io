$F(1)=\displaystyle\int_0^1(2t+\sqrt t)\,dt=\left[t^2+\tfrac23t^{3/2}\right]_0^1=\tfrac53$, y $F'(1)=3$.

**$y=\dfrac53+3(x-1)$**, es decir, $y=3x-\dfrac43$.
