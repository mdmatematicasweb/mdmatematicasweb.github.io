**a)** $f'(x)=3x^2+2ax+b$. $f(0)=c=18$; la pendiente en $P$ es $f'(0)=b=-3$; el extremo en $x=3$: $f'(3)=27+6a+b=0\Rightarrow27+6a-3=0\Rightarrow a=-4$.

**$a=-4$, $b=-3$, $c=18$**, es decir, $f(x)=x^3-4x^2-3x+18$ (que es la función $g$ del apartado siguiente).

**b)** $g(x)=(x-3)^2(x+2)$ se anula en $x=-2$ y $x=3$ (doble), y $g\ge0$ en $[-2,3]$:
$$A=\int_{-2}^{3}\left(x^3-4x^2-3x+18\right)dx=\left[\frac{x^4}{4}-\frac{4x^3}{3}-\frac{3x^2}{2}+18x\right]_{-2}^{3}=\frac{99}{4}-\left(-\frac{82}{3}\right)=\frac{625}{12}.$$
**$A=\dfrac{625}{12}\ \text{u}^2\approx52{,}08\ \text{u}^2$**

@@ 5
Sea $H$ «hipotecario» y $G$ «supera los $200\,000$ €»: $P(H)=0{,}7$, $P(G)=0{,}25$, $P(H\cap G)=0{,}2$.

**a)** $P(H^C\cap G^C)=1-P(H\cup G)=1-(0{,}7+0{,}25-0{,}2)=1-0{,}75=\mathbf{0{,}25}$.

**b)** $P(G^C\mid H^C)=\dfrac{P(H^C\cap G^C)}{P(H^C)}=\dfrac{0{,}25}{0{,}3}=\dfrac{5}{6}\approx\mathbf{0{,}8333}$.

**c)** $P(H^C\mid G)=\dfrac{P(G\cap H^C)}{P(G)}=\dfrac{0{,}25-0{,}2}{0{,}25}=\dfrac{0{,}05}{0{,}25}=\mathbf{0{,}2}$.

@@ 6

**a)** $P(V\cup L)=1-0{,}15=\mathbf{0{,}85}$.

**b)** $P(V\cap L)=0{,}65+0{,}45-0{,}85=0{,}25$, luego $P(V\cap L^C)=P(V)-P(V\cap L)=0{,}65-0{,}25=\mathbf{0{,}4}$.

**c)** $P(L\mid V^C)=\dfrac{P(L\cap V^C)}{P(V^C)}=\dfrac{0{,}45-0{,}25}{0{,}35}=\dfrac{0{,}2}{0{,}35}=\dfrac{4}{7}\approx\mathbf{0{,}5714}$.
